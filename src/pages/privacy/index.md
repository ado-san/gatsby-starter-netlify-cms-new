**Ad Ventures Limited** respects your privacy. This page explains which personal data the website **ad-ventures.cc** collects, why, and what your rights are. We follow the applicable data protection laws, including the Hong Kong Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance and, for visitors in the European Union, the GDPR.

## In short

- **No advertising, no tracking cookies, no analytics** tools on this website.
- We only receive the data you choose to send us through the contact form or WhatsApp.
- We never sell your data.

## Contact form

When you send a message through the [contact form](/contact/), we receive your **name, email address and message**. We use them only to answer you and, if we work together, to follow up on your project. The form is processed by our hosting provider, Netlify (Netlify Forms), and the notification reaches us by email.

We keep these messages as long as needed to answer you and follow up on the relationship, and at most three years after our last exchange, unless a longer period is required by law.

## WhatsApp

If you click **Chat on WhatsApp**, the conversation takes place on WhatsApp, operated by Meta, under its own privacy policy. We receive your phone number and the messages you send, and use them only to reply to you.

## Hosting and technical data

The website is hosted by **Netlify**, which serves the pages from a global network. Like any web server, it processes technical data (IP address, browser, pages requested) to deliver the pages, protect the website against abuse and produce aggregated statistics. We do not use these logs to identify visitors.

## Browser storage

Some games save your best score or your progress in your browser (local storage). This data stays on your device; we do not receive it. You can delete it by clearing your browser data. The content management system used by our editors also stores a session in the browser, only for logged-in editors.

## Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to its use, at any time through the [contact page](/contact/). If you are in the European Union, you can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

## Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
